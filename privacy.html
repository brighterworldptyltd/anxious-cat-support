Privacy Policy

Effective 30 July 2026

Your journal is stored on your device

Anxious Cat stores your cats, written entries, check-ins, photos, and voice notes locally on your device. The app has no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Permissions

The iOS photo picker lets you select individual photos without giving Anxious Cat access to your whole photo library. Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record a voice note. You can decline microphone access and keep using the rest of the app.

Free access

Every feature is free. The app has no subscription, in-app purchase, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Protection and backups

Anxious Cat applies complete iOS file protection to its local data and managed attachments. Your journal can be included in the encrypted device backups you enable through Apple. Anxious Cat does not automatically sync your journal between devices.

Your choices

Settings lets you export a local copy of your data or permanently delete all local anxiety data and managed attachments.

Support form

Contact support opens this website in your browser. The app does not attach or send your cats, entries, check-ins, photos, or voice notes. If you submit the form, it sends only the support details and optional reply email that you enter. Brighter World uses that information to respond, fix problems, and improve Anxious Cat.

Support submissions are delivered through Google Cloud, Microsoft 365, and Google email services. They are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, maintain support records, or meet legal obligations. The website and its hosting providers may process standard connection information for security and delivery. The support site has no advertising or analytics.

To ask for a support submission to be deleted, use the support form and include enough information to identify the earlier message.

Contact

Use the support form for privacy questions.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the app, support site, or data practices change.
