Privacy Policy

Effective 26 July 2026

Your journal is stored on your device

Anxious Cat stores your cats, written entries, check-ins, photos, and voice notes locally on your device. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Permissions

Photo Library access is requested only when you choose a photo. Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record a voice note. You can decline either permission and keep using the rest of the app.

Apple purchases

Lifetime Plus purchases are processed by Apple. Apple handles payment and transaction information under Apple's own privacy practices. Anxious Cat reads the verified purchase entitlement needed to unlock Plus.

Protection and backups

Anxious Cat applies complete iOS file protection to its local data and managed attachments. Your journal can be included in the encrypted device backups you enable through Apple. Anxious Cat does not automatically sync your journal between devices and does not provide its own cloud account.

Your choices

Settings lets you export a local copy of your data or permanently delete all local anxiety data and managed attachments.

Changes

This policy will be updated before the app adds any off-device collection, analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or support upload.

Contact
