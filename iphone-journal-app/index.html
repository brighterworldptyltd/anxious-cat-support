An iPhone journal app that keeps everything on your iPhone

Free on iPhone. No ads, no tracking.

Most journal apps want an account before you can write a word. Anxious Cat has no sign-in at all. You open it and start, and what you write stays where you wrote it.

Download on the App Store Free. No ads, no tracking.

Write, photograph, or speak

Each entry can hold written text, a photo, or a voice note. Voice suits the moments when typing feels like too much.

Organised by worry, not by date

Entries belong to a specific anxiety rather than to a calendar day. Months later you can open one worry and read its whole history in order, which is far more useful than scrolling through dates.

Protected on your device

Your journal is protected while your phone is locked, and you can add Face ID or passcode lock in Settings. It can be included in the encrypted Apple backups you already use.

Yours to take away

A private export gives you a single file containing your entries and media. Delete the app and its data goes with it.

Common questions

Is there a sign-up?

No. There is no account and no email required.

Does it sync between devices?

No. It does not sync automatically. Use the export if you are moving to a new phone.

Is it free?

Yes, entirely. No subscription, no in-app purchase, and no adverts.
