Purchases

Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple ID that bought Lifetime Plus.

Photos and voice notes

If a permission was declined, open the iPhone Settings app, choose Anxious Cat, and allow Photos or Microphone access.

Your local data

Anxious Cat does not have an account or cloud sync. Settings in the app includes controls to export your local data or delete it.

Urgent support

Anxious Cat does not provide medical, crisis, or emergency support. Contact local emergency services or a qualified professional when immediate help is needed.
