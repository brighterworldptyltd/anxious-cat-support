An anxiety tracker that shows you what actually happened

Free on iPhone. No ads, no tracking.

Tracking anxiety is only useful if it tells you something you did not already know. Anxious Cat tracks two things together: how bad a worry felt at the time, and what actually happened afterwards.

Download on the App Store Free. No ads, no tracking.

Intensity at the moment it matters

Rate a worry from 1 to 5 when it is live, not at the end of the week when the feeling has faded. The cat for that anxiety changes with the rating, so intensity is something you see rather than a number you have to interpret.

The follow-up is the point

Check in later and record the outcome: it got better, it stayed the same, it got worse, or it ended. Most trackers stop at how you felt. Recording the outcome is what turns a diary into evidence.

Patterns you can actually see

Categories, intensity over time, and resolved against active worries build up into a picture of which situations keep returning and which ones resolve on their own.

Nothing is uploaded

There is no cloud account and no analytics. What you track stays on your iPhone, and you can export or delete all of it whenever you want.

Common questions

Do I have to track every day?

No. There are no streaks and no guilt mechanics. You add a cat when something is worrying you and check in when you are ready.

Can I add photos or voice notes?

Yes, and both stay on your device.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings has a private export that produces a single file you keep.
