Studies and Sources

Anxiety predictions

In one small study, 29 adults with generalized anxiety disorder recorded their anxious predictions for 10 days and tracked what happened for 30 days. Across the recorded predictions, 91.4% did not come true.

This result comes from that study. It is not a result measured in Anxious Cat, and it does not mean the app will reduce anxiety by 91.4%.

LaFreniere LS, Newman MG. Behavior Therapy. 2020. View on PubMed.

Breathing

The app includes a simple 5-second inhale and 5-second exhale guide. Breathwork research includes different exercises, durations, and populations. Anxious Cat does not claim that this exact exercise provides a specific medical result.
